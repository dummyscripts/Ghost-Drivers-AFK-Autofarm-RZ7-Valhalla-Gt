## Start driving

1. Join Ghost Drivers with a supported owned car near the highway route and execute the command.
2. Select **Get key**, choose **LootLabs**, **Work.ink** or **Linkvertise**, complete the provider's tasks in the same browser, then paste the issued key into the script and select **Unlock**. Paid keys are optional.
3. Select your car while driving is **OFF**. Choose **Cruise**, **Fast**, **Max** or a custom dashboard MPH target. Switch **ON** to start and **OFF** to stop.

**F8** stops driving and restores tracked paint changes. **Right Shift** hides or shows the panel by default; change this in Settings. Before updating, press F8, wait for cleanup, then execute the same loader again.

## Features

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Driving | Continuous ON/OFF autofarm, physical route assistance, recovery from recoverable interruptions, speed presets and custom MPH targets. |

Paint changes your **local view** and is not a saved game upgrade. Visibility to other players is not guaranteed. Paint runs independently of driving: switching Driving OFF leaves an active effect running. Rainbow OFF, Restore original or F8 restores tracked original colors. Active effects do not automatically resume after reloading.
